## 🚀 Overview

**Pranalyse** is an AI-powered health-tech platform that transforms any smartphone or laptop into a **real-time movement coach**.

Unlike traditional fitness apps, Pranalyse doesn’t just show exercises—it **analyzes posture, corrects movements instantly, and guides users step-by-step**.

Built for **beginners, students, professionals, and rehabilitation users**, it ensures fitness is **safe, personalized, and judgment-free**.

---

## ❗ Problem

Millions of people follow online fitness, yoga, and rehab content daily—but without knowing if they’re doing it correctly.

This leads to:

- ❌ Incorrect posture  
- ❌ Injuries & chronic pain  
- ❌ Poor or no results  
- ❌ Loss of confidence  
- ❌ High drop-off rates  

> 👉 Most users quit not due to lack of motivation—but due to **lack of guidance and fear of doing it wrong**.

---

## 💡 Solution

**Pranalyse** solves this with **real-time AI posture correction and personalized wellness guidance**.

Using just a camera, it:
- Detects body posture using **33-point pose tracking**
- Provides **instant, easy-to-understand feedback**
- Acts like a **virtual trainer + physiotherapist**
- Tracks progress and builds long-term habits
